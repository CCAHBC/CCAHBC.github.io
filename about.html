Overview

Join the human behavior club!

CCA’s Human Behavior Club is a club that focuses on collecting and analyzing data gathered from conducted social experiments. The goal of the club is to understand the behaviors of humans and the reasons behind their decisions.

This is a new, student-led club that was created by and for people that share a passion for psychology and are interested in conducting their own research and coming to conclusions themselves! We will be interviewing students at the school and participating in experiments like the Street Corner Experiment. This club is a fun, collaborative way to sate your own curiosity about human behavior.

This club is open to any and all students who are interested, and it would be a great opportunity to make friends with similar passions. No prior experience with psychology is required to join.

Meeting Details:
CCA’s Human Behavior Club will meet every Monday at lunch in room C104. Anyone is welcome to stop by!

If you are looking for a low-pressure, fun way to participate in researching the very behaviors exhibited here at CCA, this is the club for you. We are very eager to gain more members to have bigger projects and we are always open to feedback!

This club’s main goal is to offer an entertaining and educational experience for those who are looking for opportunities to explore the field of psychology or neuroscience further.
